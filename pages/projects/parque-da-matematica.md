Parque da Matemática is an educational game about mathematics, developed at [LabTIME](https://labtime.ufg.br), the research and game development laboratory of the Federal University of Goiás.

## My work

- **Puzzles and gameplay**: programmed the math puzzles and core gameplay.
- **Build pipeline**: automated builds and asset processing with RegEx-driven scripts.
- **XR**: XR systems and optimized rendering.
