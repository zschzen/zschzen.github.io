The Ashes of Jorge is a 2D side-scrolling beat 'em up, produced in one month (May to June 2019) as the Projeto Integrador II of the Digital Games program at Faculdade Senac Goiás.

The brief was strict: mechanics grounded in the classic [beat 'em up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_%27em_up) genre, with an aesthetic inspired by [Scott Pilgrim vs. the World: The Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Pilgrim_vs._the_World:_The_Game).

Made by a team of three: Dyego Marques on design, art, characters, and animation; game design, programming, and documentation by me and Geovani Ildefonso.

## Tech

- **Unity 2019.1** with **C#**
- Full project documentation in the repo: game design document, people management, and schedule (it was graded, after all)
