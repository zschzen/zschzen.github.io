Game da Cidadania is an interactive activity from the civic education department of Brazil's Controladoria-Geral da União (CGU), developed in partnership with [LabTIME](https://labtime.ufg.br) at the Federal University of Goiás. It is offered free of charge online, and asks adolescents and young adults to reflect on the ethical and civic dilemmas that come up in everyday life.

- **Shader programming** for the game's visuals.
