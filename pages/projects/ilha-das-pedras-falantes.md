Ilha das Pedras Falantes ("Island of the Talking Stones") is an educational game developed at [LabTIME](https://labtime.ufg.br), the research and game development laboratory of the Federal University of Goiás.

## My work

- **Gameplay** programming.
- **GPU optimization**: reworked HLSL/Cg shaders for a 20% frame-rate boost.
- **Cutscenes**: bug fixes and general debugging.
- **Virtual production**: the capture pipeline with Blackmagic Ultimatte 12, Xsens, Vicon and Faceware.
